Selected work

Five to start with

01 Full-stack product workshop

Skill checks scored like chess ratings

SkillCompass

SkillCompass gives two-minute quizzes in more than 40 fields and scores each answer Elo-style against the item's difficulty. You get a percentile against other test-takers, a map of what you know and a study plan. The backend is FastAPI on free tiers, so it costs nothing to keep running.

SiteWrite-upCode

02 Decision tool workshop

Route planning for a five-year-old battery

Leeway

Leeway plans from the range your car has now, after five years of battery wear, and adjusts it for hills and weather. It keeps a hard reserve at every charger stop and spends a detour budget you set on avoiding unprotected left turns and rail crossings.

SiteWrite-upCode

03 Survival analysis track

How long does a world record live?

World-Record Half-Lives

I treated 248 track-and-field record reigns as survival times, with the records still standing as censored. Kaplan-Meier puts the median reign at about 1.5 years, and a Cox model gives each current record its odds of falling.

SiteWrite-upCode

04 Regression road

A hot day costs the typical Boston runner 31 minutes

The Heat Tax

Fourteen Boston Marathons paired with the temperature on the course that day. The field's median finish slows about a minute per degree Fahrenheit (r = 0.86, with the interrupted 2013 race left out), so a 50°F year and an 80°F year are half an hour apart.

SiteWrite-upCode

05 Language app language

Learning spoken Cantonese from today's news

Cantonese Learner

Every day an LLM rewrites Hong Kong headlines from written Chinese into the Cantonese people speak, and a second pass reviews it. Each line comes with neural zh-HK audio and jyutping, plus tap-to-compare playback and speech grading.

SiteWrite-upCode

Wine Score Inflation cellar

The 100-point wine scale is an 8-point scale

Across 130,000 professional reviews nothing scores below 80, and 86% land between 84 and 92. Some critics are simply more generous than others.

Hitting the Wall road

The wall arrives between 20 and 35 km

Pace drifts from 10 km on, then falls off in the second half. The median runner's late-race pace is 15% slower than their early pace.

Are Song Lyrics Getting Simpler? language

Song lyrics got longer, not simpler

Fifty years of Billboard hits doubled in length and grew more repetitive, while the vocabulary stayed about where it was.
